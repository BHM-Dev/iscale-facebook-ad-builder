Which to pick
Option A — Inline click-to-edit
+ Fastest for Joel who knows his numbers
+ No extra click to open a panel
− CBO/ABO toggle has no natural home here
− Easy to misclick the number while scanning
Option B — Budget button + popover
+ CBO/ABO toggle lives right here
+ Less accidental edits
+ Shows current budget prominently on button
− One extra click vs inline
Recommendation: Option B — the CBO/ABO toggle needs a home, and a button that shows the current budget ($500/day) is more scannable than a plain clickable number.
